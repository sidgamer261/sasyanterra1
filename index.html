Hardware subsystems

Every module built to service and scale.

Modular V-slot aluminum architecture lets us swap end-effectors, service electronics and upgrade payloads in minutes rather than days.

  • SYS // 01 · ENCLOSURE

    Faceted weather-sealed hull

    Low-profile composite fairing shields internal electronics from dust, spray and field debris, accented by high-visibility cyan optical status strips.

  • SYS // 02 · MANIPULATOR

    Carbon-truss laser arm

    Lightweight truss boom with braided internal routing, bronze-anodized wrist collar, optical targeting pod and interchangeable pruning or gripper tools.

  • SYS // 03 · AVIONICS

    Dual-board AI & sensor stack

    Dedicated edge compute and motor-control PCBs paired with front optical/LiDAR sensing, GNSS/IMU fusion and dual high-density battery packs.

  • SYS // 04 · PAYLOADS

    Fluid tank & gear-metered hopper

    Side translucent micro-dosing tank and a rear transparent granular hopper with precision spur-gear metering for targeted seeding or nutrition.

  • SYS // 05 · SPINE

    Extruded V-slot structural spine

    Rigid T-slot aluminum backbone with active finned heatsink cooling and quick-release access to batteries and power distribution.

  • SYS // 06 · MOBILITY

    6WD carbon rocker-bogie & planetary hubs

    Six independent high-torque planetary hub drives mounted on CNC carbon-fiber suspension links with stainless steel turnbuckles and beadlock all-terrain tires—keeping all six wheels planted over ruts, stones and wet soil.

Field thesis

Farming doesn’t need heavier machines. It needs smarter ones.

From sub-surface soil diagnostics to laser-guided pruning and zero-compaction 6WD mobility, Sasyanterra replaces brute-force guesswork with targeted precision.

The vision · Sasya + Terra

What if every farm had a tireless autonomous partner? One that reads the living soil, spots obstacles early, and lends a precision arm.

Why Sasyanterra exists

Named from Sanskrit Sasya (सस्य — crop and harvest) and Terra (living earth), Sasyanterra tackles the heavy, repetitive labour of everyday farming. Our mission is to reduce manual strain, monitor crop and soil health continuously, and bring aerospace-grade robotics within reach of real farms.

What Unit-02 delivers

Sasyanterra v2.0 unites six-wheel all-terrain mobility, multi-sensor obstacle perception, soil diagnostics, dual dispensing hoppers and a laser-guided robotic arm in one modular platform—streaming clear insights and recommendations straight to your phone.

Questions

Plain questions, straight answers.

Have a technical question or farm use-case? Message us directly and the team will answer.

What is Sasyanterra?

Sasyanterra (v2.0 // सस्यनंतेरा) is an AI-assisted, six-wheel-drive multipurpose agricultural rover. It combines carbon rocker-bogie mobility, multi-sensor perception, a laser-guided robotic arm, dual payload hoppers and a companion mobile app for smarter, more sustainable farming.

Can I buy or pilot one right now?

Not commercially yet—Sasyanterra is currently an active working prototype (Unit-02). If you’d like to follow development, collaborate or discuss farm trials, message us on WhatsApp.

What can the robotic arm and hoppers do?

The carbon-truss manipulator supports interchangeable tools such as laser-guided pruning shears and grippers, while the translucent side tank and rear gear-metered hopper support targeted fluid and granular dispensing.

What does the Sasyanterra mobile app do?

It brings field telemetry from the rover straight to your phone—displaying soil conditions, flagged obstacles, UGV status and practical crop-care recommendations.

Who is behind Sasyanterra?

Sasyanterra is led by Sai Siddarth (Team Leader), Rian (Assistant Team Leader) and Pranaouv (Developer), focused on making high-capability agricultural robotics practical and accessible.
