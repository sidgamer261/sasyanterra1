# Sasyanterra v2.0 (`SYT // सस्यनंतेरा`)

Premium interactive static website for **Sasyanterra v2.0 (Unit-02)** — an autonomous 6WD agricultural UGV combining carbon rocker-bogie suspension, a laser-guided manipulator arm, multi-sensor perception, dual payload hoppers, and live mobile telemetry.

## Highlights

- **Brushed-Titanium `SYT` Brand Identity**: Custom-traced vector & raster `SYT` monogram (`assets/logo/syt-logo.jpg`, `assets/logo/syt-mark.png`, `assets/favicon.svg`, `assets/share.jpg`) with 3-piece boot rise, periodic specular light sweep, and 3D hero tilt card.
- **Interactive Hero Dot Field & Shockwave (`#top`)**: Cursor-reactive dot grid with click/badge shockwave ripples and live IST telemetry strip.
- **Studio Light-to-Obsidian 299-Frame Cinema (`#anatomy`)**: Interactive scroll & scrub breakdown revealing all 6 internal hardware chapters.
- **360° Orbital Subsystem Ring (`#ring`)**: 3D perspective carousel orbiting 6 subsystem cards around the central `SYT` core.
- **3D Perspective Scroll Corridor (`#corridor`)**: 3,850px Z-axis fly-through across floating agronomic & hardware thesis cards into the `SYT` manifesto.
- **Draggable 3D `SYT` Engineering Cube (`#story`)**: Interactive 6-faced 3D block synchronized with the 5-stage engineering loop.
- **Autonomy & Telemetry Particle Reactor (`#reactor`)**: Live Bézier packet simulation (`Ingest → Filter → Fuse AI → Actuate`) with spark bursts and live HUD counters.
- **Interactive Robotics Lab Wall (`#lab`)**: 6 live 60fps studies (`Hardware Burst` mosaic explosion, `LiDAR Magnet` dot field, `Sub-Hull Scanner` X-Ray wipe lens, `Brushed SYT Slab` 3D tilt, `Canopy Parting` kinetic type, and `Serpentine Row` path scrub) with fullscreen `<dialog>` expansion.
- **Live Signal, Analogue IST Dial & Rule Breaker (`#signal`)**: Workshop status feed, SVG analogue India clock + global ag-belt clocks, and 5-mode kinetic letter explosion stage.
- **1,100-Dot `SYT` Mutation, Split & Explosion Stage (`#mutation`) & Dot-Matrix Footer Signature**: Full-screen particle stage that warps around the pointer, detonates/morphs shapes on click, splits into 6 sectors on scroll, and converges into the `SYT` monogram.
- **Guided Briefing Drawer & WhatsApp Dispatch (`+91 95007 02412`)**: 4-step keyboard-friendly overlay plus direct forms and trilingual support (`EN` / `தமிழ்` / `हिन्दी`).
