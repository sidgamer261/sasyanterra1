Privacy

Last updated November 2026

This is an informational project website for Sasyanterra (SYT · v2.0 // सस्यनंतेरा). It uses no user accounts, no backend database and no advertising or tracking cookies.

What we collect

Nothing is stored by this website itself. The “Send a note”, “Guided briefing” and “Apply to join” flows format a message locally in your browser and open WhatsApp, where you choose whether to press send.

Direct messages & calls

When you message us on WhatsApp, call us or interact on social platforms, those services operate under their own privacy policies. We use the details you share solely to reply to your enquiry or review your interest in collaborating.

Fonts & language preference

Typography loads from Google Fonts. If you switch the site language to Tamil or Hindi, that preference is saved only in your own browser’s local storage.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete any message history you have shared with us by contacting 95007 02412 on WhatsApp.
